# Camera Movement Skill (运镜)

Purpose: choose and describe ONE motivated camera move per shot so AI video models
(Seedance, Veo, Kling) execute it cleanly. The user often cannot prompt movement — do the
craft for them. Movement must be *reasonable*: it exists to serve the beat, never as decoration.

## The one-move rule (最重要)
- Exactly ONE primary camera move per shot. Never stack moves (no "orbit then push-in then
  dolly-zoom"). Stacked moves make the model jitter, warp, and lose the subject.
- Do not add a second move the brief did not ask for. If the brief's move is static, keep it static.
- Never tangle camera motion and subject motion in one clause — describe the subject's action,
  then the camera's move, separately.

## Name the speed, default slow
- Every move states a speed and easing: default **slow, ease-in/ease-out**. Slow moves render
  cleanly; "fast" is the single most quality-degrading word — reserve it only for an intentional
  whip transition. A move with no named speed is an error.

## Emit this structure for the chosen move
move type → speed + easing → start framing → end framing → lens → camera height/angle →
stability (tripod / gimbal / handheld) → the motivation (why this move serves THIS beat).

## Move vocabulary — motivation + default speed
- **Static / locked-off** — the camera holds; the SUBJECT carries the motion. Best for dialogue,
  intimacy, quiet beats, macro detail. A still camera over a moving subject is correct, not a dead
  frame. Prefer it whenever the performance is the point.
- **Slow push-in (dolly-in)** — tension, importance rising, emotional focus. Slow, eased.
- **Slow pull-out (dolly-out)** — reveal context, endings, loneliness. Slow, eased.
- **Slow dolly / truck L–R** — reveal space and geography; needs foreground/mid/background layers.
- **Tracking** — travel WITH a moving subject (walking, action); match the subject's pace.
- **Slow tilt up/down** — reveal height, scale, significance. Slow across the full range.
- **Slow pan** — scan a scene or follow lateral movement; keep it gentle, not a whip.
- **Subtle handheld drift** — realism, documentary immediacy, intimacy. Small natural sway only.
- **Crane up/down** — grandeur, solitude, fate, scale. Dramatic — use when the beat earns it.
- **Aerial / drone drift** — landscape and scale. Dramatic — use when the beat earns it.
- **Orbit / arc** — signals a HERO / central / important subject: a reveal, an introduction, a power
  beat. Dramatic — use only when earned. A partial arc reads calmest; a full 360° is available for a
  genuine epic reveal or an environment sweep, but keep it a SINGLE slow move, never welded to a push.
- **Whip-pan** — only as a hard transition between beats; the one place speed is intentional.
- **Rack focus** — the camera stays locked; the FOCUS plane shifts from one subject to another
  (foreground → background, or object → face). A motivated "move" that redirects the audience's
  eye and reveals a relationship without any camera travel. Ideal when you want a still frame to
  still guide attention. Counts as the shot's one move; name the start and end focus subjects.

## Match the move to the shot
- The move must complete inside the shot's length — a slow push over 3s reads differently than
  over 8s. State a pace the duration can actually carry; do not cram a full 360° into a short beat.
- Start and end framing should match the keyframe the shot begins on, so the move continues the
  still rather than fighting it.

## When NOT to move
If the beat is a performance or an intimate exchange, hold the camera and let the actor move — or
rack focus to redirect the eye. Movement with no dramatic reason is the main defect to avoid.

## Negatives (motion defects)
stacked/conflicting camera moves, unmotivated orbit, spatial warping from over-fast or over-wide
moves, jitter, temporal flicker, camera drift with no reason, subject and camera motion tangled.

# 运镜技能（中文）

目的：为每个镜头选择并描述**一个**有动机的运镜，让 AI 视频模型（即梦/Veo/可灵）干净地执行。
运镜必须**合理**：它服务于叙事节拍，绝不是装饰。用户往往不会写运镜，替他们做好这门手艺。

## 单一运镜原则（最重要）
- 每个镜头只用**一个**主要运镜。绝不堆叠（不要"环绕+急推+变焦"）。堆叠会让模型抖动、变形、丢失主体。
- 不要擅自增加简报未要求的第二个运镜。若简报是固定机位，就保持固定。
- 不要把运镜和主体动作写在同一句里——先写主体动作，再单独写镜头运动。

## 标注速度，默认慢速
- 每个运镜都要写明速度与缓动：默认**慢速、缓入缓出**。慢速画面更干净；"快速"是最容易劣化画质的词，
  只在有意的甩镜转场时使用。没有速度的运镜是错误。

## 为所选运镜输出以下结构
运镜类型 → 速度+缓动 → 起始构图 → 结束构图 → 镜头焦段 → 机位高度/角度 →
稳定方式（三脚架/云台/手持）→ 动机（为何此运镜服务于本节拍）。

## 运镜词表——动机 + 默认速度
- **固定/锁定机位**——镜头不动，由**主体**承担运动。适合对话、亲密、安静时刻、微距细节。
  固定机位拍摄运动的主体是正确的，不是死板画面。表演是重点时优先使用。
- **缓慢推近**——紧张、重要性上升、情绪聚焦。慢速缓动。
- **缓慢拉远**——揭示环境、结尾、孤独。慢速缓动。
- **缓慢横移/平移**——揭示空间与地理，需要前中后景层次。
- **跟拍**——跟随运动的主体（行走、动作），速度与主体一致。
- **缓慢俯仰**——揭示高度、体量、重要性，缓慢走完全程。
- **缓慢摇镜**——扫视场景或跟随横向运动，柔和，不是甩镜。
- **轻微手持晃动**——真实感、纪实即时感、亲密。仅小幅自然晃动。
- **升降镜（摇臂）**——崇高、孤独、宿命、体量。较戏剧化，节拍配得上时才用。
- **航拍/无人机漂移**——风景与体量。较戏剧化，节拍配得上时才用。
- **环绕/弧线**——标示**主角/核心/重要**主体：揭示、登场、力量时刻。较戏剧化，仅在配得上时使用。
  部分弧线最平稳；完整 360° 可用于真正史诗级的揭示或环境展示，但要保持**单一**慢速运镜，绝不与急推捆绑。
- **甩镜**——仅作节拍间的硬转场，是唯一有意使用速度之处。
- **变焦移焦（rack focus）**——机位锁定不动，**焦平面**从一个主体转到另一个（前景→背景，或物体→人脸）。
  这是一种有动机的"运镜"：不移动机位就重新引导观众视线、揭示关系。想让固定画面仍能引导注意力时最理想。
  算作本镜头的唯一一个运镜；写明起始与结束的对焦主体。

## 让运镜匹配镜头
- 运镜必须在镜头时长内走完——3 秒的缓推与 8 秒的缓推观感不同。写明时长能承载的速度；不要把完整 360° 塞进短节拍。
- 起始与结束构图应与镜头开始的关键帧一致，让运镜延续静帧，而不是与之冲突。

## 何时不动镜头
若节拍是表演或亲密交流，就锁定机位，让演员去动，或用变焦移焦重新引导视线。没有戏剧理由的运镜是最主要的缺陷。

## 负面词（运动缺陷）
堆叠/冲突的运镜、无动机的环绕、过快或过大运动导致的空间变形、抖动、时间闪烁、无理由的镜头漂移、
运镜与主体动作纠缠。
