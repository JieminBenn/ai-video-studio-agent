# Location Identity Skill

Purpose: create stable environment anchors so backgrounds, props, lighting, and spatial logic stay consistent.

Use this structure:

- Location concept: what the place is, why it matters to the story, and the emotional pressure it creates.
- Spatial layout: entrances, exits, foreground/midground/background zones, hero angles, and screen direction.
- Architecture and materials: walls, floor, ceiling, windows, weathering, texture, signage, and surface behavior.
- Hero props: objects the audience must recognize later, including their exact position and state.
- Palette and light states: stable colors, practical lights, time of day, motivated key light, shadows, haze, weather.
- Continuity rules: what cannot move, disappear, flip direction, or change color across shots.
- Establishing reference: one clean image that defines the place.
- Environment board: wider layout, close-up material/prop panels, lighting notes, and color swatches.

Prompts should make the location feel designed, inspectable, and reusable rather than generic scenery.
Avoid vague backdrops, impossible room geometry, inconsistent windows/doors, random prop changes, and unmotivated light shifts.
